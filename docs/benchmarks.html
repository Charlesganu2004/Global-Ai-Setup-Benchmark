        Findings

        In plain sentences, with their numbers.

        • On the light class, one agent using "Our engine, first generation, with embeddings" needed 130k tokens and 2.0 turns for the three lookups, against 365k tokens and 5.0 turns with no index: 64% fewer tokens.
        • 3 of 3 runs were fully correct with "Our engine, first generation, with embeddings" and 4 of 5 with no index. A run is fully correct when all three answers are.
        • Two turns is the floor for this task: one to ask and one to answer. On the light class 1 of 6 published configurations took exactly two turns and answered everything correctly in every run: "Our engine, first generation, with embeddings".
        • Tokens follow turns. On the light class the leanest published configuration took 2.0 turns and 130k tokens, the heaviest 7.0 turns and 480k tokens.
        • With no index, one agent used 365k tokens on the light class, 281k tokens on the mid class, 298k tokens on the top class; the answers were 98%, 100%, 100% correct. A larger model class did not need far fewer tokens for the same lookups.

        Orchestration

        With a reviewing orchestrator on top of three light workers, 48 of 48 runs ended fully correct, against 98 of 106 for one light agent alone; a run cost about 95 times as much.

        • Fully correct, orchestrated48 of 48
        • Fully correct, one light agent98 of 106
        • Cost of one runabout 95 times

        Tool hooks

        Tool hooks that compress and redact reads did not lower token use on these lookups, and without an index fewer runs ended fully correct (5 of 9 runs with the hooks on, 13 of 17 with them off).

        • Fully correct, hooks on5 of 9
        • Fully correct, hooks off13 of 17
        • Change in token use+2%
